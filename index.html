Direct Layover took cuePlacer from concept to beta launch, leading product strategy, naming, brand identity, UX/UI, animation, software development, testing & release planning, alongside trademark & IP planning.

The Windows & Mac app turns audio into precise, editable cues; iOS is in early development. The Windows build combines C#, WebView2, and a JavaScript interface with custom waveform rendering, playback-linked markers & export workflows.

Built for editors, directors, motion designers, lighting designers, show programmers & choreographers working across film, advertising, music videos, live shows, theatre, dance & installations.

A kick that lands. A snare that snaps. The split second a track goes quiet.

cuePlacer lets you see what you hear. Press play, mark the sounds that catch your ear, and fine-tune each cue down to the millisecond. If you can tap along, you can use cuePlacer.

Maybe you can already picture the angle changes, quick zooms, and cuts in your new music video and want to share that sequence with your editor. Or maybe your lighting designer’s flight was canceled, and you need to show them exactly where the show’s new cues land while they’re still on another continent. Lay out those moments on the timeline, then export your cues to supported software or share them as a timing guide. Everyone can see exactly when each change is meant to happen, so you spend less time going back and forth on “a little earlier” and more time making it work.
